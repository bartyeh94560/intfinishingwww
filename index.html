Where Modern Finish Science Meets Old-World Hand Artistry

Founded in 1993 in Rohnert Park, California, Interior Finishing Inc. was established to provide the North Bay and greater San Francisco architectural community with a dedicated finishing partner capable of meeting the highest standards of luxury residential estates, boutique hospitality, and commercial architectural showcases.

Whether your project demands precision-controlled shop finishes or rigorous field work on historic timbers, our dual-environment workflow ensures flawless color matching, durable finish chemistry, and seamless coordination with your site schedule.

State-of-the-Art Shop

Clean-room spray booths, controlled drying, fuming chambers, and specialized glazing stations.

Mobile On-Site Crew

Field artisans trained for architectural beams, existing trim integration, and final hand wax & buffing.

How We Partner with Project Teams

From schematic design to on-site punch list, we maintain rigorous control over every coat.

01

Scope & Specification

We review architectural elevations, species specifications, required wear ratings, and project milestone dates.

02

Sample Iteration

03

Precision Shop Coating

04
